## 1.0.0

* First release: highlight overlay, 6 tooltip presets, arrow and dots pointer, per-page progress with resume, journeys, central JSON config, SharedPreferences and SQLite storage, progress import/export and sync.
* Light and dark mode (`darkTheme`, `themeMode`, per-level `dark` overrides), `barrierOpacity`.
* Diamond and beveled highlights, dashed and gradient borders, `highlightOffset`.
* Custom pointer painter, per-dot colours and dot shapes.
* iOS and Android transition tests and an on-device integration test.
* Fewer rebuilds and repaints while a step is on screen.
* Works on the web out of the box: SQLite storage is only used where it is available.
